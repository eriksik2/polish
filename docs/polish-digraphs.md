# Polish Digraphs — Reference for the App

> **Sources:** [Wiktionary: Polish pronunciation](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Appendix:Polish_pronunciation), [Wikibooks: Polish pronunciation](https://en.wikibooks.org/wiki/Polish/Polish_pronunciation)

## Overview

Polish has **7 digraphs** — two-letter combinations representing a single sound:

**ch, cz, dz, dź, dż, rz, sz**

## Digraph table

| Digraph | IPA | English approximation | Same as | Example words |
|---------|-----|----------------------|---------|---------------|
| **ch** | /x/ | ch as in Scottish *loch* | **h** | chleb, brzuch |
| **cz** | /tʂ/ | ch as in *child* (hard) | — | czas, cześć |
| **sz** | /ʂ/ | sh as in *shore* (hard) | — | szum, szkoła |
| **rz** | /ʐ/ | zh as in *measure* | **ż** | rzeka, marzec |
| **dz** | /dz/ | ds as in *beds* | — | dzban, dzwon |
| **dź** | /dʑ/ | j as in *jeep* (soft) | — | dźwig, dziadek |
| **dż** | /dʐ/ | j as in *jug* (hard) | — | dżem, dżungla |

## Voicing pairs (hard)

| Voiceless | Voiced |
|-----------|--------|
| c /ts/ | dz /dz/ |
| cz /tʂ/ | dż /dʐ/ |

## Soft pair

| Voiceless | Voiced |
|-----------|--------|
| ć /tɕ/ | dź /dʑ/ |

## Identical sounds, different spelling

- **ch = h** (same /x/)
- **rz = ż** (same /ʐ/ in most positions)
